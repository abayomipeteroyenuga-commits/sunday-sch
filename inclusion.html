CLEAR · FLEXIBLE · WELCOMING

Inclusive Teaching

Use practical design choices that make participation easier without singling learners out or requiring private disclosure.

Visual Schedule

Show the class order: welcome, Bible, discussion, activity, prayer, close.

Clear Instructions

Give one or two steps at a time and repeat instructions calmly when needed.

Multiple Ways to Respond

Allow speaking, writing, pointing, drawing or paired discussion where appropriate.

Readable Visuals

Use large text, strong contrast and uncluttered slides.

Quiet Participation

Do not require every learner to speak publicly or disclose personal experiences.

Predictable Transitions

Tell learners what is ending and what comes next before changing activities.

Short Movement Breaks

For longer classes, use brief age-appropriate movement between learning blocks.

Coordinate With Caregivers

Where appropriate, ask caregivers or church safeguarding leaders about helpful participation supports.
